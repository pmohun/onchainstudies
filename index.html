A sample field guide: Ways of Seeing

Draft guide / for Phil’s review

What have you stopped noticing?

Why this belongs. Berger asks how the context around an image shapes what we see in it. This is a book to keep near your everyday encounters with pictures, possessions, and advertising.

Where to begin. Read the opening essay, then spend time with the images. Choose one familiar advertisement and ask what it invites you to want.

Its neighbors. Feynman approaches the world by trying things and asking questions. Berger asks you to question the act of looking itself. King brings that attention to the page: how reading, noticing, and practice shape a writer’s craft.

One question to keep. What do I see here, and what have I been taught to see?

Who it suits. Readers curious about art, images, and culture. It is a critical argument, rather than a practical design manual; agreeing with every claim isn’t the point.

Make it yours. Write down one image you now read differently, and one point you disagree with. Keep the note inside the cover.
