A NOTE ON THE SHELF

What belongs
beside what?

Hamming asks what deserves your attention. Scott asks what your systems leave out. Steinbeck asks you to sit with the people inside them.

I choose books with a thread between them. Each arrives with a one-page field guide: why this book now, what it belongs next to, and a question to take with you.
