What we decide this round

Pick the strongest direction. Name one element to keep and one to remove. A hybrid can come next, once the underlying structure is clear.

  1. Would you recognize it without the name?
  2. Can you see the curation—not just the subscription?
  3. Can you find what you get and how to start?
  4. Does it hold up on a phone?
